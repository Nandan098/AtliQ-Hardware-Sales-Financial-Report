<p>
This project analyzes <b>4 years of sales and financial data</b> for AtliQ Hardwares using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor sales performance, profitability, customer trends, and market growth across multiple fiscal years.
</p>

<p>
Using <b>Power Query</b> for ETL, <b>Power Pivot</b> for data modeling, and <b>DAX</b> for KPI calculations, the project delivers executive-level insights that support strategic business decisions.
</p>

<h2>Business Insights</h2>

<ul>

<li>Sales demonstrated steady growth across multiple fiscal years, indicating expanding market demand.</li>

<li>A limited number of products generated a significant share of total revenue, highlighting key revenue drivers.</li>

<li>Several low-performing products contributed minimal sales, presenting opportunities for portfolio optimization.</li>

<li>Market performance varied across countries, with some regions consistently exceeding sales targets while others require strategic improvements.</li>

<li>Gross Margin % fluctuated by quarter, revealing seasonal trends and operational efficiencies.</li>

<li>Customer purchasing analysis identified repeat high-value customers contributing substantially to total sales.</li>

</ul>
